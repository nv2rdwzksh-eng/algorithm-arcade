# Sorting Lab design

Operate mode: the visualization is the primary working surface. A warm paper ground, restrained forest-green controls, spacious chart and ruled explanatory notes keep operation state legible. Compared bars use amber, modified bars coral, final positions green. Markers and text supplement color. Manrope headings and DM Sans body have sans-serif fallbacks; fonts are optional network enhancement.

Use one experiment panel with integrated transport and statistics. Notes occupy a secondary column on desktop and flow beneath on mobile. Native controls, explicit labels, visible focus, reduced-motion support. No decorative imagery or additional UI dependencies.
